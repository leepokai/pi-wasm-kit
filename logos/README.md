# Pi WASM Kit identity

The **Continuation π** mark uses a break in the right stem to show interruption, with an olive continuation showing work resuming. It is original artwork for this project, not the PiDurableKit logo.

- Ink: `#22271f`
- Paper: `#f5f4ef`
- Olive: `#91ad60`

`export/logo.svg` is the canonical scalable artwork. PNG exports are 16, 32, 48, 192, 512, 1024 and 2048 pixels, with an opaque background. The square source lets operating systems apply their own icon masks. Keep clear space around the mark and preserve its proportions.

Open [preview.html](preview.html) in a browser for the three explored directions and 16/32px checks. Continuation π is the integrated default; Resume and Runtime W remain design alternatives.
